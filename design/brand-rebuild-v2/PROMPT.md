# Team 47 Studio — Brand Rebuild v2: maximized prompt

Version 2.0 · 2026-11-14 · This is the brief the rebuild was executed against.

## Role and goal
Lead brand and product designer for Team 47 Studio (MGT Solutions, Inc. d/b/a Team47Studio). Rebuild the web portal and app interface as 10 conceptually different, end-to-end designs, all built on the established logo and its colours, each working in light and dark mode. Optimize for customer appeal, ease of use, instant comprehension and low-cost marketing across digital channels, partner portals and campaigns.

## Fixed brand inputs (do not redesign)
- **Logo:** the established prism "47" mark + "Team Studio" wordmark. Use the light-surface file on light grounds and the dark-surface file on dark grounds. "Team" and "Studio" must be clearly legible in both: logo on a solid ground only, minimum 44 px tall in the app and 50 px on the web, never recoloured, stretched, cropped or placed on a tint, gradient or image.
- **Palette from the logo:** navy #200068, blue #1E88F8, violet, with cyan, lime and amber from the mark as secondary accents. Violet stays the single action colour.
- **Membership:** Free, Guided, Studio, using the tier wording in `packages/shared/src/membership.ts`. Show a price only where one is confirmed; otherwise a visible [PRICE] placeholder.

## Context to load first
Memory file for the platform; the live Design canvas (merge, never overwrite the owner's edits); Drive logo kit and Team 47 Studio documents; the portal repo's active branch (brand assets, membership tiers); Brand Variations v1.0 workbook (principles P1–P10 and scores).

## What "10 different" means
Each design has a different organizing idea for the experience, not a different skin: what the visitor meets first, and what the app home leads with. Example ideas: profile-as-prism, question-first, routine-first, coach-first, colour-season-first, shop-first, editorial, data-transparent, card grid, calm minimal. Typography, shape language and density follow the idea.

## Scope per design (end to end)
- **Web portal:** homepage, Skin Match result before sign-up, signed-in portal home, membership page.
- **App:** welcome, Skin Match step, result, home, routine, shop, makeup shade match, hair-colour lift check, style guidance, coach preview, coach chat, profile.
- Full journey in the design's primary mode, plus web home and app home in the other mode to prove the logo and palette hold in both.
- Shared content spine so designs compare on design, not copy.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Cosmetic guidance only; no diagnosis, treatment or medical claims; keep the line "Cosmetic guidance, not diagnosis or medical treatment."
- No body weight, measurements or body-type inputs or wording in Style and Clothing.
- Lift check: amber never red, lead with what the user can do, no shame wording, no route to the unsafe product, name a recheck date, salon guidance above products.
- Make no privacy or data-retention claim the product does not verifiably keep.
- Six-tab app bar: Skin, Makeup, Hair, Style, Coach, Profile.
- Inspiration only from reference sites; nothing recognisable from another brand.
- Accessible as drawn: 4.5:1 text contrast, 44 px touch targets, real buttons and links, no fake device status bars.

## Outputs
1. **Design canvas:** one page per design, earlier rounds kept as an archive page.
2. **Google Drive:** a dated folder with a PDF lookbook, a workbook (overview, one tab per design, logo rules, membership), the source boards and this prompt.
3. **GitHub:** a new branch on the portal repo (never main) holding the boards, design tokens as JSON per design and mode, the generator, the logo files used and a README. No pull request unless asked.

## Working rules
Mark assumptions inline as [ASSUMPTION]; separate sourced facts from estimates. Decide and continue unless a choice is irreversible. Check before finishing: every board renders, the correct logo variant is on every board, 10 designs × 18 boards exist, Drive and GitHub contents match the canvas.

## Final report (short)
Links, a one-line idea per design, what changed from the last round, open decisions, anything not reachable.

## 1. What month 1 is for

Month 1 spends $2,000 to answer one question: which concept, on which placement, brings a Starter sign-up or a trial start at a cost that can scale. The step-up rule from the addendum is the only success metric: **one concept per channel with CTR ≥ 1.2% and CAC ≤ $95**. Everything else is learning.

Channel split: Meta $1,200 (Facebook feed, Instagram Reels, Stories), TikTok $800 (Spark Ads on creator clips). No Google in month 1.

Benchmarks the performance agent judges against (retrieved 2026-11-15; secondary trade sources, not platform rate cards): Meta beauty CPC $0.22 Facebook feed, $0.77 Instagram Reels, $0.71 Stories, $0.94 Instagram feed (Emplifi Q3 2026); Meta beauty CTR 1.9–2.8% (Flighted 2026); TikTok beauty CPC $0.58–$0.85 and CTR 1.0–2.0% (Benly / bir.ch 2026); creative fatigue sets in at about 8.4 days (AdRiseLab, H1 2026).

## 2. Rules every ad obeys

1. **Fit, never outcome.** "Your shade, matched." is allowed. "Clears", "repairs", "reduces", "heals", "treats", "anti-" anything is not.
2. **No "accurate", "precise", "scientific".** The colour-season thresholds are not yet calibrated (marketing §4.2).
3. **No timing claim.** "About a minute" is unsourced. Say "a few questions".
4. **No before-and-after imagery, real or illustrated. No faces.** Creative is colour, swatches, palettes, product tiles and the interface.
5. **No real brand names** until clearance. Catalogue tiles use the invented names in the demo.
6. **Trial ads state the price after the trial and that it renews**, in the ad itself, not only on the landing page: "14 days of Guided, $0 today. Then $14.99 a month, cancel any time."
7. **Targeting is interest and lookalike only.** No skin-condition, health or appearance-based audiences. Meta restricts health targeting and the claims rules forbid it anyway.
8. **Every ad carries the short required line** in the last frame or the caption: *Guidance for looking and feeling your best — cosmetic, not medical. Results differ from person to person.*
9. **Landing page is the pre-signup Skin Match result**, never a sign-up wall. The result is the product; the ad promises the result.

## 3. Concepts

Two concepts per channel, each in two variants, so month 1 tests eight ads. All use the segment ramps and Prism Weave from marketing §5; type is Space Grotesk 700 for headlines, DM Sans for everything else.

### Concept A · "Twelve tones" (coverage, swatch-first)

**What it shows.** The swatch ladder sliding from light to genuinely deep tones, then the palette that comes back for one of them. Prism Weave Night behind it. No face.
**Hook (first 2 seconds):** "Twelve tones. Not six."
**Body:** "Pick the swatch closest to you. Get the palette that fits, and why."
**Headline:** Your shade, matched.
**Primary text (Meta):** Twelve tones, six verticals, one profile behind all of it. Pick your swatch and see your match before you make an account.
**CTA button:** Find my match
**Variant A1:** 9:16 Reel, 12 s, swatch ladder animates upward, palette lands at 6 s, required line at 10 s.
**Variant A2:** 1:1 static carousel, three cards: ladder / palette / "shows its work" explanation panel.
**Why it should work:** coverage is a fact we can show (marketing §4.1), and the deep end of the ladder is a visible difference from the category norm.

### Concept B · "Shows its work" (method, trust)

**What it shows.** A Coach answer on screen, with the "what I used / what I left out" lines highlighted, then the lift-check refusal screen returning no products. Prism Weave Day, light ground.
**Hook:** "It tells you what it did not use."
**Body:** "Every suggestion names what it is built from. Sometimes the right answer is no products at all."
**Headline:** Made to fit you, not an average.
**Primary text:** Guidance that can show its work: what it used, what it left out, and where it is not sure. Built from your own profile.
**CTA button:** Find my match
**Variant B1:** 9:16 Reel, 15 s, screen recording style with captions.
**Variant B2:** 4:5 static, the refusal screen with the amber "not yet, and here is how long" copy.
**Why it should work:** the category is full of outcome promises; a refusal is a credible, unusual thing to put in an ad, and it is already true of the product.

### Concept C · "Begin free" (TikTok, Starter door)

**What it shows.** Creator (hands and product only, no face) choosing swatches on a phone, the season landing, the starter routine appearing step by step. Spark Ad on the creator's own post.
**Hook:** "No card. No clock. Just your match."
**Body (creator voice, plain):** "You answer a few questions, pick the swatch that is closest to you, and it builds a routine with a reason for every step. Starter is free to keep."
**On-screen text:** Begin free. Grow when it suits you.
**CTA:** Find my match
**Variant C1:** 18 s, native vertical, trending-safe audio (licensed library only).
**Variant C2:** 12 s, same footage, cut to the routine reveal.

### Concept D · "14 days" (TikTok, trial door; runs only after O-7)

**What it shows.** Creator hands opening the Guided routine, the progress journal, and the Coach, ending on the $0-today checkout screen with the renewal line visible.
**Hook:** "Fourteen days of Guided. Nothing today."
**Body:** "Routines that update every two weeks, a journal, and a coach that names what it used. Fourteen days to decide. Then it is $14.99 a month, and you can cancel yourself."
**On-screen text:** 14 days of Guided, $0 today. Then $14.99/mo. Cancel any time.
**CTA:** Try Guided for 14 days
**Variant D1:** 20 s. **Variant D2:** 12 s, journal-led.
**Compliance note:** the price-after-trial and renewal line is in the video and the caption, not only on the landing page (rule 6).

## 5. What the performance agent does, daily

1. Pull spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, Starter sign-ups, trial starts and CAC per ad set from the Meta and TikTok APIs and the member database.
2. Flag any ad past day 8 with CTR down 20% from its peak as **fatigued**; propose the next variant from the queue.
3. Flag any ad set with CAC above $130 for 14 consecutive days as **kill candidate**. Flag any concept with CTR ≥ 1.2% and CAC ≤ $95 as **scale candidate**.
4. Post one summary a day to the owner: three lines, the numbers, the flags, the proposed moves. Never move budget, pause or launch anything itself.
5. Weekly: compare against the benchmarks in §1 and the step-up rule; state plainly whether month 2 spend is earned.

## 1. Cadence and why

Instagram does nearly all the organic work in beauty: the median beauty brand's Instagram post earns about 40 interactions against about 5 on Facebook (Emplifi, Q3 2026). So the calendar is Instagram-first, TikTok second, Facebook a mirror only.

| Every week | What | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 5 short videos | One per vertical, rotating (skin, makeup, hair, hair colour, style/wardrobe), 9–18 s, no faces | Instagram Reels and TikTok (same cut, native captions) |
| 1 carousel | "Shows its work": one Coach answer or one refusal, explained in three cards | Instagram feed, mirrored to Facebook |
| Daily Stories | One frame a day from the product: a swatch pick, a palette, a routine step, a filter, a poll | Instagram Stories |
| Reposts | Any paid ad that passes the step-up rule (CTR ≥ 1.2%, CAC ≤ $95) goes up organically within 48 hours | Reels and TikTok |

Post times: 11:00 and 18:00 Eastern on weekdays for videos; Stories spread through the day; carousel Thursday 12:00. Adjust after week 2 from the account's own insights, not from a benchmark.

Organic is modelled in the plan as a 15–35% multiplier on paid trial starts, not as a channel on its own. The job of month 1 is a consistent, on-rules feed that a paid visitor can land on and trust.

## 2. Rules every post obeys

Same nine rules as the ad briefs §2, plus three for organic:

1. **No faces, no before-and-after, no real brand names.** Creative is swatches, palettes, the interface, hands and product tiles with invented names.
2. **Fit and method, never outcome.** "Your shade, matched." Never clears, repairs, reduces, heals, treats, or "accurate".
3. **No engagement bait.** No "comment YES", no "tag someone who needs this", no giveaways in month 1.
4. **No reposting of member content** until a UGC rights process exists.
5. **No replies that give personal cosmetic advice in comments or DMs.** The care chatbot answers with the Skin Match link and the required line; anything medical-adjacent goes to a person.
6. **Every caption ends with the short required line** and no hashtag walls: three to five hashtags, all generic (no brand or competitor tags).

Approved hashtags: #colouranalysis #colorseason #skincareroutine #hairandcolour #wardrobepalette. Nothing else without approval.

## 4. Measurement

The performance agent adds organic to its daily pull: reach, saves, shares, profile visits and link taps per post, and intake starts and trial starts attributed to the link-in-bio parameter. The only month-1 organic target is consistency (all 20 videos, 4 carousels and 28 Stories posted on schedule); reach and engagement set the baseline for month 2. Any post with saves above twice the account median is queued for a paid test under the ad briefs.

You draft organic captions and cut lists from SOCIAL-CALENDAR-M1-v1.0.md.

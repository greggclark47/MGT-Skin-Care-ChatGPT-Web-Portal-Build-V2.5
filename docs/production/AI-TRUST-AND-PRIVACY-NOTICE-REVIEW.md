# AI trust and privacy notice review

The customer facing drafts at `/trust`, `/privacy`, and `/ai-disclosure` describe the current MGT Skin Care web portal and Android scope in plain language. They are designed to appear together with the existing Terms review draft and Account & Customer Care routes.

## Included product statements

- Cosmetic guidance, routines, ingredient education, color matching and anti aging content do not provide diagnosis or treatment.
- AI can organize a question, explain reviewed content, and draft cosmetic or style ideas. It cannot make a health, payment, retailer, routine change, or provider decision for the customer.
- Separate analysis permission is available in the account and can be withdrawn.
- Color match uses only self selected appearance and style fields needed for that request. Wardrobe notes are excluded and optional journal photos are not used for that feature. A separate photo-journal cosmetic note remains disabled by default; when both privacy gates are approved, it accepts one explicitly selected prepared JPEG for a local-only vision task with no hosted fallback, no identity or facial recognition, no health inference, and no retained image bytes, filename, Drive path, or model text in the account trace.
- Retailer links do not transmit a customer’s profile, skin details, wardrobe or photos to the retailer.
- Account controls include export, analysis permission, and a deletion request with a 30 day cancellation period before processing begins.
- Android release configuration requires HTTPS Privacy and Terms URLs. Its current release surface has no photo, location, contacts or financial data permissions.

## Publication inputs

The legal and business owners must approve the legal entity, contact route, effective date, supported territories, age rules, processors, data transfer locations, retention schedules, deletion exceptions, security incident contact, final billing processor terms, and the availability of any hosted AI provider. The published Privacy notice must be checked against the final Android build, SDK inventory, payment setup and provider data processing agreements.

## Drafting references

The drafts draw on the transparency, explainability, privacy and risk documentation practices described in [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework), the [NIST generative AI profile](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf), and the [ICO guidance on AI transparency](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/guidance-on-ai-and-data-protection/how-do-we-ensure-transparency-in-ai/). These sources inform the language; they do not replace legal review for the launch jurisdictions.

# Account, customer care and terms review

The web portal now routes account setup, profile preferences, Premium billing, subscription controls, image management, routine questions, retailer handoffs, privacy rights, accessibility and technical issues through `/care`. Existing authenticated controls and support tickets remain the action points. Support request links preselect only approved request types. No support delivery or billing activation is implied by a saved request.

`/terms` contains a customer readable review draft matched to the current web and Android feature boundaries. It does not present itself as an effective agreement. Counsel and the business owner must supply and approve the following before publication:

1. Exact contracting entity, business and legal notice addresses, supported territories, minimum age and effective date.
2. A final Privacy notice with processors, image storage, retention, regional rights, transfers, incident contact and deletion exceptions.
3. The actual Premium offer: price, currency, tax treatment, billing cadence, trial/promotion details, renewal disclosures, cancellation path and refund policy. Verify the checkout and subscription screen show these before payment.
4. Product referral and paid placement disclosures adjacent to recommendations when any commercial arrangement becomes active.
5. Approved cosmetic claims, AI disclosure, image consent, intellectual property, acceptable use, suspension, availability, warranty and liability language, governing law and dispute process, subject to mandatory consumer rights.
6. A staffed support contact and escalation route, plus accessible policy publication on web and Android.

Reference points used for the draft: [FDA cosmetics claims](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-labeling/cosmetics-labeling-claims), [FTC subscription guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/getting-and-out-free-trials-auto-renewals-and-negative-option-subscriptions), and [FTC endorsement guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking). These references guide drafting; a qualified reviewer must resolve the law and policies for the selected launch territories and actual offer.

# MGT Skin Care merchant feature and omni-retail outreach packet

Prepared: November 3, 2026  
Status: working outreach packet - legal, privacy, commercial, and brand review required before use

## Purpose

This packet gives MGT Skin Care a consistent, permission-first way to invite a merchant, brand, or authorized retailer to feature approved products in the MGT web portal and Android-linked experience. It is designed for a referral model: the merchant remains the seller of record and the customer completes payment, fulfillment, returns, and merchant support on the merchant's site.

This is not a signed agreement, a promise of placement, legal advice, or a production launch authorization. Replace bracketed fields, obtain authorized signatures, and have counsel review the final agreement and disclosures for each launch territory.

## 2. What MGT is requesting

For each approved merchant or brand, MGT requests written confirmation of the following scope:

1. Permission to display the approved merchant or brand name, product name, approved description, approved product imagery, and official product or store link.
2. Permission to feature only the approved catalog scope in MGT's discovery, routine, education, retail, and marketing surfaces.
3. Permission to direct a shopper to the merchant's verified external storefront for purchase.
4. Permission to identify the merchant relationship accurately as Independent listing, Preferred partner, or Sponsored placement, as agreed.
5. Permission, if separately agreed, to receive aggregate reporting about external-link opens and to submit aggregate conversion or routine-category volume reports to MGT.

No permission is inferred from a public product page, prior email discussion, a social-media mention, or a listing in the directory. MGT will not use a merchant's trademark, product imagery, feed, or promotional claims beyond the approved scope.

## 4. Commercial and customer-boundary terms

1. **Merchant of record.** The merchant is the seller of record and owns pricing, stock, tax, payment, delivery, returns, refunds, warranties, and merchant customer support.
2. **External purchase.** MGT provides an external link only. MGT does not take a customer's card, create a merchant order, hold inventory, or promise merchant availability.
3. **No hidden promotion.** Independent listings retain their ordinary directory order. Preferred partner and Sponsored placement labels are disclosed before the shopper leaves MGT.
4. **No protected or health claims.** Merchant content must be substantiated, permitted in the launch territory, and consistent with MGT's cosmetic-education boundaries. MGT may reject or pause a claim pending review.
5. **No automatic routine changes.** A click, reported purchase volume, or placement status cannot automatically add, remove, replace, or override a customer's routine, sensitivity setting, product avoidance, or professional-care boundary.
6. **No customer data exchange by default.** MGT does not send a customer's profile, image, health information, wardrobe, saved routine, payment information, cart, email, or identity to the merchant through a referral link.
7. **Review and removal.** Either party can request correction, removal, or pause of an asset, link, product, territory, or placement. MGT will define response timing and will pause unsafe, expired, or disputed material.

## 5. Aggregate attribution and routine-category volume

MGT's initial partner design has two deliberately separate measures:

1. **Portal outbound engagement.** MGT may count an aggregate outbound open by merchant and shopping segment. It is not a unique-customer count, an order, revenue, conversion, or profit figure.
2. **Merchant aggregate report.** After approval, a merchant may submit a bounded report for a defined period containing only attributed order count, attributed unit count, and optional unit totals by routine category (for example cleanser, treatment, moisturizer, or sunscreen).

The aggregate report must not contain customer identifiers, emails, addresses, IP addresses, payment data, itemized receipts, order IDs, web cookies, browsing activity, raw cart data, health information, or free-text customer notes. MGT treats it as an operational planning input only. It may inform aggregate catalog coverage or education planning, but not individual routine recommendations or customer profiling.

Before activation, document:

- Attribution method and approved parameters
- Reporting cadence and time zone
- Start and end dates for each report
- Deduplication and correction method
- Definition of cancelled, returned, or adjusted volume
- Data-retention period and deletion / correction contact
- Authorized reporting sender and verification method

## 6. Technical onboarding checklist

1. Verify that the merchant controls the external destination domain.
2. Select approved product or collection URLs and check they use HTTPS.
3. Provide product identifiers, title, image, description, ingredient or category data, price-band, availability cadence, and territory limitations as agreed.
4. Validate that links do not carry MGT customer identifiers or sensitive profile data.
5. Test web and Android referral handoffs in a non-production environment.
6. Confirm the merchant support, returns, privacy, and terms URLs that will appear near the link.
7. Agree on visible placement label and promotion period.
8. If aggregate reporting is enabled, test a synthetic report with no customer data and confirm idempotent correction handling.
9. Obtain product, marketing, privacy, accessibility, and operations sign-off.
10. Publish only after the agreed record, disclosures, support handoff, and rollback / pause contact are complete.

## 7. Marketing and placement playbook

### Independent listing

Use for editorial or discovery listings without a commercial agreement. The page should say "Independent listing" and preserve neutral directory order. Do not imply endorsement or track merchant conversions as a partnership metric.

### Preferred partner

Use only after written authorization and a reviewed commercial agreement. The page must say "Preferred partner" before an outbound click. A preferred relationship can support a dedicated merchant feature, approved campaign content, and aggregate reporting, but it cannot suppress safety information or change personal routine output.

### Sponsored placement

Use only for a paid or sponsored campaign with legal review and customer-visible "Sponsored placement" disclosure. Maintain a separate campaign scope, dates, assets, applicable territories, archive, and pause contact. Do not represent sponsorship as personalized suitability, clinical support, or a cosmetic outcome guarantee.

# Team 47 Studio experience and GTM direction

Status: **product and experience direction**. This is not approval to publish,
contact prospects, enable paid media, or activate sponsored placement.

## Experience architecture

The portal home now presents the product in the order a person is likely to
need it instead of leading with a broad application directory:

1. **Personal Studio** — begin with Skin Match, then return to routine,
   progress, replenishment, and account controls.
2. **Curated Discovery** — explore anti-aging education, color, wardrobe, and
   external retail destinations with source and commercial labeling intact.
3. **Partner Collaboration** — give prospective retailers and professionals a
   transparent partnership and support path without implying automatic data
   sharing, lead delivery, or purchase processing.

The complete application hub remains available below these paths for people
who know exactly where they need to go. This keeps the complex product
discoverable without forcing a new visitor to parse every capability first.

## High-end UI principles

- Start with one clear decision and reveal product depth in layers.
- Treat profile, routine, image, consent, and billing choices as personal
  controls rather than conversion obstacles.
- Keep retailer destinations external and label partner or sponsored context
  where it appears.
- Pair each consequential action with an explanation, reversible choice, or
  visible support route.
- Preserve keyboard, reduced-motion, forced-color, touch-target, semantic
  heading, and status-announcement support as non-negotiable experience work.

## Go-to-market architecture

| Journey stage | Customer experience | Partner/support role | Measurement boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Editorial landing and Skin Match invitation | Brand and approved content review | Consent-aware aggregate reach only |
| Evaluate | Explainable routine, ingredient, and style context | Reviewed education and clearly labeled referrals | Profile completion and feature engagement, not health claims |
| Continue | Routine, progress, replenishment, and care access | Customer care resolves requests and documents replies | Support responsiveness, retained-use signals, opt-in feedback |
| Partner | Partner page and approved collaboration workflow | Human-reviewed partner outreach and placement approval | Aggregate placement and referral reporting only |

## Initial channel posture

- **Owned:** portal landing pages, educational discovery, lifecycle messages
  only after preference and consent controls are active.
- **Partner-led:** approved retail co-marketing, retailer landing pages, and
  human-reviewed outreach. No scraping or automatic contact workflow.
- **Paid or sponsored:** disabled until commercial authorization, disclosure,
  measurement, support, and placement review are complete.

## Campaign inputs required before execution

The implementation intentionally does not invent campaign economics or launch
claims. The campaign owner must define these inputs before a channel plan is
scheduled:

1. primary objective (awareness, qualified leads, subscription trials,
   retailer partnerships, or retention);
2. audience priority and geography;
3. launch date and campaign duration;
4. approved media and production budget; and
5. support capacity, legal/disclosure approval, and attribution requirements.

With those decisions, create a channel-specific content calendar, asset plan,
targets, and reporting cadence. Until then, all outreach remains draft-only
and no customer or partner action is automated.

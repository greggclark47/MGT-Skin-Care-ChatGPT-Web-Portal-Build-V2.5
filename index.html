How does billing work?

Premium uses one approved billing product with separate monthly and annual rates. Checkout creates a pending request; access changes only after a signed billing event is reconciled.

Can I cancel?

The portal can request end-of-period cancellation or renewal. The change remains pending until the signed billing event confirms it.

Does MGT process product purchases?

No. Skincare product purchases remain with the external retailer. Premium is the only planned MGT consumer charge.

Is Premium available now?

Not in this demo. Enrollment remains unavailable until commercial, legal, support, and billing readiness are complete.

How will billing work?

Your first subscription payment date starts the billing cycle. Monthly plans renew monthly; annual plans renew annually and include two months free. Secure billing shows charges, credits and any renewal-date change before a plan or cycle switch is confirmed.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Account management lets you move between available plans and monthly or annual billing. The effective date and any prorated amount are shown before confirmation.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel anytime from Account. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period, and access continues through that date.

How long is inactive account data retained?

Customer data may be retained for up to 24 months to support a return. A verified deletion request overrides that window after the recovery period, except for limited legal, security or accounting obligations.

Can I enroll now?

No subscription can be started from this preview.

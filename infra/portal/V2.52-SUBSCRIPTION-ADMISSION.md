# v2.52 subscription admission checkpoint

Review date: 2026-10-12. Accountable owner: Gregg Clark of MGT.

Production startup now requires an exact subscription feature declaration. Disabled subscription scope remains valid without payment credentials. Enabled scope requires explicit approved terms, an exact live/test mode, a mode-matching Stripe secret reference, a signing-secret reference, one Premium product, and distinct monthly and annual Price identifiers.

Invalid or cross-wired billing configuration fails before database construction. This does not prove a Stripe account, prices, webhook delivery, commercial approval, or customer billing behavior; those remain live evidence.
